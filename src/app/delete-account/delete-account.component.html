Account Deletion Request

ANAAD Foods Mobile App

We respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. If you wish to delete your ANAAD Foods account and all associated data, you can submit a request through our secure form below.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

Once your account deletion request is processed:

  • Personal Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and delivery addresses will be permanently removed from our systems.
  • Order History: All your past order records will be deleted.
  • Subscription Data: Any active subscriptions will be cancelled and their records removed.
  • Preferences: Your saved preferences, favorites, and wishlist items will be erased.
  • Cart Items: All items in your shopping cart will be removed.

Important Information

  • Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once processed, you will not be able to recover your account or any associated data.
  • If you have any pending orders, please wait until they are delivered before requesting account deletion.
  • Processing time for deletion requests is typically 7-14 business days.
  • You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been successfully deleted.
  • After deletion, you can create a new account using the same email or phone number if you wish to use ANAAD Foods services again.

Data Retention

Some information may be retained for legal or regulatory compliance purposes, such as transaction records required for tax purposes. This retained data will be anonymized and will not be linked to your personal identity.

Considering Other Options?

Before deleting your account, you might want to consider these alternatives:

  • Pause Subscriptions: If you need a break, you can pause your subscriptions temporarily instead of deleting your account.
  • Update Preferences: If you're receiving unwanted communications, you can update your notification preferences in the app settings.
  • Contact Support: If you're facing issues with our service, our support team would be happy to help resolve them.

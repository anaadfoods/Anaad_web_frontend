The Origin

Three decades of chemicals,
three years to undo them.

When ANAAD began farming this land, the goal was not just to grow a crop. It was to bring the soil back to life after years of chemical-dependent farming that had drained its natural strength.

That transformation did not happen overnight. It took three full growing cycles, or about one and a half years, of patient observation, natural inputs, and consistent care to help the soil become alive again. What stands today is not just a farm in production, but land that has slowly regained its breath, its biology, and its ability to nourish a crop the way healthy soil should.

What ICBN farming actually means

ANAAD follows a farming method built on living soil, on-farm fertility, and long-term crop integrity. Each principle is simple, direct, and designed to create food that is more traceable, more dependable, and more nourishing.

Principle 01

Living soil first

Healthy crops begin with healthy soil. The first priority is to build a soil system rich in microbial life so the crop can access nutrition naturally and grow with greater balance and resilience.

Principle 02

On-farm fertility

Fertility is built on the farm using indigenous cow-based preparations rather than relying on external chemical inputs. This keeps the farm ecosystem more self-sustaining and more closely connected to the land it grows from.

Principle 03

Seed integrity

The farm begins with carefully chosen traditional seed lines suited to the land, the season, and the food they are meant to become. Strong seed integrity helps preserve resilience, consistency, and the character of the crop from one generation to the next.

Principle 04

Microbial abundance

A thriving microbial population is essential for real soil fertility. When microbes are abundant, they help unlock nutrients, improve soil structure, and support stronger, more balanced crop growth.

Principle 05

Nutrition with integrity

Every farming decision is made to protect the nutritional value of the crop. The aim is not just yield, but grain that carries the full value of the soil, the seed, and the care invested into it.

Principle 06

Traceable by design

The growing system is built so food remains connected to its source. That makes the harvest easier to trust, easier to understand, and easier to trace back to how it was actually grown.

The Living Engine

The living machine: our herd of 7 indigenous cows.

At the center of the farm is a herd of seven indigenous cows whose dung and urine form the foundation of ANAAD's biological preparations. These preparations help rebuild soil life, improve nutrient access, strengthen plant health, and reduce dependence on toxic farm inputs.

Preparation Details & Benefits
Gaukripaamrutam
Liquid Bacterial Culture 		Gaukripaamrutam is used to rapidly break down toxic residues left behind by past synthetic fertilizer use while introducing trillions of beneficial bacteria into the soil. It helps unlock phosphorus, potassium, and zinc into plant-available forms, while also accelerating humus formation and increasing soil carbon.
Ghanjeevamrutam
Solid Organic Manure 		Ghanjeevamrutam works as a slow-release source of nutrition throughout the crop cycle. It improves soil structure, supports moisture retention, attracts native earthworms back into the topsoil, and is easy to apply during land preparation or as top-dressing.
Anaerobic Jeevamrutam
Anaerobic Jeevamrutam 		Anaerobic Jeevamrutam is a concentrated microbial broth created through oxygen-free fermentation. It helps suppress harmful soil-borne pathogens, supports stronger root branching, and delivers natural enzymes and growth-promoting compounds that improve crop quality and vigor.
Anaerobic Bio-Pesticide
Jaivik Kitnashak 		This farm-made bio-pesticide uses fermented botanical extracts to naturally repel common pests such as sucking insects, caterpillars, and beetles. It remains safe for beneficial insects like honeybees and butterflies, supports the plant's own defense system, and reduces the need for expensive toxic sprays by replacing them with farm-sourced ingredients.
From Our Soil to Your Kitchen

From our soil to your kitchen

What begins in living soil should reach your kitchen with the same integrity it was grown with. That is why ANAAD builds every part of the journey around traceability, thoughtful handling, and nutrition that stays closely connected to the farm.

The idea is simple: when you choose food from ANAAD, you should be able to know where it came from, how it was grown, and why that matters for what you feed the people at home.

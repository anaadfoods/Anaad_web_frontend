Vedic Calendar

We farm by the sky, not just the soil.

The Panchang — the Vedic agricultural calendar — governs every sowing, every incorporation, every harvest on our farm. Not as ritual. As science refined over four thousand years of agrarian observation.

Modern agriculture runs on quarterly profit cycles. ICBN farming runs on a much older clock — one that tracks lunar phases, asterisms, and seasonal transitions that have governed Indian harvests for millennia. We make our calendar public because transparency means showing you not just what we grow, but when and why.

The Vedic calendar is an empirical record — generations of farmers observing correlations between celestial cycles and plant behaviour, codified into a system that modern biodynamic research increasingly validates. For instance, sowing during specific lunar phases directly correlates with enhanced seed germination rates and optimum sap flow.

What is the RFP

Farming Without
Leaving Home

The ANAAD Remote Farming Program is a structured partnership that lets urban families sponsor, oversee, and receive produce from dedicated farm plots — without relocating. You choose your tier, we manage the land, and your family eats the result.

Every plot is farmed using ICBN (Indian Cow Based Natural) methods — indigenous cow breeds, zero synthetic inputs, Jeevamrit-treated soil, and nitrogen-flushed packaging at dispatch. Your food is grown as India grew it for 5,000 years.

"Your own farm. Your own batch number. Your own food story."

The Four RFP Pillars

Every Remote Farming Program tier is built on these four non-negotiable commitments.

📋 Accountability

Direct Accountability

You know the name of the farmer growing your food. You receive monthly field reports, crop photos, and soil health updates directly — not filtered through a PR team.

🌏 Soil Health

The Earth Audit

Twice-yearly independent soil health audits by SGS India. You receive the full lab report — nitrogen levels, organic carbon, microbial activity — not a cherry-picked summary.

🔍 Transparency

Radical Transparency

Every batch from your plot gets its own registry entry. Scan the QR code on your package and trace your grain from seed variety to sowing date to harvest weight to your doorstep.

🌿 Priority Access

Early Product Access

RFP members receive new heirloom varieties and seasonal releases before public availability — with first right of refusal on limited batches from their dedicated plot.

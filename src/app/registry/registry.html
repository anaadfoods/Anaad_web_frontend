Trace your food from field to shelf.

Product Registry

At ANAAD, trust is built on transparency. That means every product you receive can be traced back to the field where it was grown, the batch it came from, and the care that went into making it.

This registry is not about a single certificate. It is about sharing real records for every batch of every product we offer. From grain to flour and any other items we produce, you will find batch-level details that show where the food came from and how it was handled.

We believe trust should be downloadable.

Every batch record is available to download. You can access field details, handling steps, and batch information in a file you can keep, share, or verify at any time.

Batch Ledger

The Batch Ledger is the central record for all ANAAD products. It lists every batch across all product lines, not just grain. Each entry includes the field where the product was grown, the harvest date, processing details, packaging information, and the path it followed before reaching you.

How We Mill

How your flour is prepared

01

Harvested in season

The wheat is harvested during the crop season and brought in carefully once it is ready.

02

Stored with care

After harvest, the grain is stored properly so its quality remains protected over time.

03

Orders are received

When an order comes in, it is added to the upcoming milling schedule.

04

Milled as scheduled

The wheat is milled into flour according to planned milling slots so it can be prepared in an organized and efficient way.

05

Vacuum packed

Once milled, the flour is packed in vacuum packaging to support a natural shelf life and protect product quality.

06

Dispatched to you

After packing, the order is dispatched. Subscriptions make this process easier to plan, helping ANAAD mill flour more smoothly against expected demand.

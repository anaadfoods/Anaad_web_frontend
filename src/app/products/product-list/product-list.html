How We Mill

How flour used to be: whole, fresh, nutrient‑rich

01

Harvested in season

Harvest happens only when wheat has naturally ripened at the end of the rabi season, ensuring full grain maturity and maximum nutrition..

02

Stored with care

After harvest, the grain is stored properly so its quality remains protected over time.

03

Orders are received

When an order comes in, it is added to the upcoming milling schedule.

04

Milled as scheduled

The wheat is milled into flour according to planned milling slots so it can be prepared in an organized and efficient way.

05

Vacuum packed

Once milled, the flour is packed in vacuum packaging to support a natural shelf life without using toxic industrial preservatives.

06

Dispatched to you

After packing, the order is dispatched, subscriptions make this process possible to plan, helping anaad farmers to exclusively grow and mill for subscribers only Subscriptions make this process easier to plan, helping ANAAD mill flour more smoothly against expected demand.

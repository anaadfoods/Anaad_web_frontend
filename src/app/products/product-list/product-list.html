Processing Method

What happens between harvest and your kitchen.

01

Harvest at Optimum Moisture

Grain is cut at 12–14% moisture content to prevent post-harvest degradation. Threshed within 24 hours to preserve enzymatic activity in the germ.

02

Natural Sun Drying

Grain is dried on raised bamboo platforms for 3–5 days. No mechanical dryers — no heat exposure before milling that would pre-oxidise grain oils and denature enzymes.

03

Cold Stone Milling

Our on-farm granite stone mill operates below 40°C throughout the milling process. Modern roller mills run at temperatures that destroy wheat germ oil, B vitamins, and heat-sensitive micronutrients. We do not use them.

04

Nitrogen Flush Packaging

Flour is immediately packed into food-grade kraft bags with a nitrogen flush, displacing oxygen before sealing. This prevents oxidation and rancidity — giving our flour a stable 9-month shelf life without any preservatives.

05

72-Hour Dispatch

Every bag is dispatched within 72 hours of milling. We do not stock finished flour. We do not mill in advance. What you receive is genuinely fresh.

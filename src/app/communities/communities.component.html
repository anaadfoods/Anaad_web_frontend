KRINITY

The Farmer's
Brotherhood

KRINITY — from Krishi (agriculture) — is ANAAD's exclusive network for farmers practicing or transitioning to ICBN (Indian Cow Based Natural) farming. Share wisdom, get support, find your tribe.

For farmers practising or exploring ICBN methods
Direct channel to ANAAD's agronomy experts
Verified network — each member's land is traceable
Priority access to contract farming opportunities

What You Get in KRINITY

5 pillars of the farmer network

1
Share ICBN Techniques Exchange field-tested methods for Jeevamrit preparation, Beejamrit seed treatment, and natural pest management with fellow ICBN practitioners.
2
Get Real Insights Access crop calendars, soil health data, and weather-adjusted guidance built on real farm outcomes from ANAAD's network — not textbook theory.
3
Find Farming Brotherhood Connect with farmers across Haryana, Punjab, and beyond who share your commitment to soil-first agriculture. You are not alone in this work.
4
Connect with Experts Regular AMAs and virtual field visits with soil scientists, ICBN specialists, and ANAAD's own agronomy team — ask what you need, directly.
5
Boast Your Harvest Showcase your best yields, share your batch registry links with consumers, and receive community recognition for exceptional produce quality.

GRANITY

The Conscious
Consumer Circle

GRANITY — from Grahak (consumer) — is where families who care about what's on their plate come together. Share recipes, question labels, swap health stories, and hold the food system accountable.

For families seeking real, traceable, heirloom food
Private, curated — no influencer noise
Behind-the-scenes access to ANAAD's farm operations
Early product drops before public launch

What You Get in GRANITY

6 pillars of the conscious consumer circle

1
Conscious Families Be part of a vetted circle of families who have made the commitment to eat real food. Shared values, shared stories, real conversation — no brand noise.
2
Heirloom Recipes Discover and share recipes built around ANAAD's seasonal grains — from Emmer khichdi to Kodo millet kheer. Community-curated, kitchen-tested.
3
Health Stories Real accounts from GRANITY members on how switching to heirloom grains has affected digestion, energy, and long-term wellness — evidence over anecdote.
4
Nutrition Discussions Deep-dive threads on the science of ancestral nutrition — glycaemic index, phytate content, traditional fermentation, and what modern food processing strips away.
5
Lifestyle Support From meal planning around your CBA box to transitioning children to natural foods — community support for every step of the real-food journey.
6
Behind-the-Scenes Access Live farm visits (virtual and in-person), pre-harvest dispatches, batch photos, and direct Q&As with the farmers who grew your food.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How do I track my order?

Log in to your account and visit the Orders section. Each order has a live tracking timeline from farm dispatch to your doorstep. You'll also receive SMS and email updates at every stage.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?

Yes — you can pause, skip a dispatch, or cancel your CBA subscription at any time from the Subscription Details page in your account. No lock-in, no penalty.

What is ICBN farming and how is it certified?

ICBN stands for Indian Cow Based Natural farming — a regenerative method using local cow breeds' dung and urine. Our produce is SGS India certified for residue-free status after a 90-day monitoring period.

Do you accept bulk or institutional orders?

Yes, we work with restaurants, health clinics, and corporate wellness programmes. Email us at connect@anaadfoods.com with your requirements and we'll share our wholesale pricing sheet.

What is your return / refund policy?

If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, raise a complaint within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We replace or refund within 5 business days — no questions asked.

How do I join the Remote Farming Program?

Visit the RFP page and choose your tier — either the Family Mini Farm or Contract Farming with ANAAD. Submit an inquiry form and our team will contact you with next steps within 72 hours.

The Origin of ANAAD.

We returned to Toxin-Free ICBN practices not to build a business, but to secure our own survival. ANAAD is simply us sharing that lifeline with you.

The Vision: Food Sovereignty.

“

We didn't get into farming to build a mass packaged-goods brand. We got into it because we couldn't find food in the market that we could trust completely for our own kids.

”

A few years ago, I started looking into where our daily staples—atta, rice, and dal—actually come from. Like most families, we bought whatever was labeled 'organic' or 'healthy' and assumed it was good for us. But the more I looked, the more concerned I became.

Most flour on retail shelves is milled months before it reaches your kitchen. To keep it from going bad, the grain is stripped of its nutrient-rich germ, and flours are stored in warehouses treated with chemical fumigants. Even 'whole wheat' is typically ground in high-speed industrial rollers that generate intense friction heat, scorching the natural oils and live enzymes. In short, we were paying high prices for stale, warehouse-stored flour.

If we couldn't trust what was in the market, we had to grow it ourselves.

I knew that buying through brokers or putting our brand name on someone else's harvest wouldn't fix this. Real quality only happens when you are personally accountable for the soil, the water, and the seed.

Back to the farm in Sonipat

In 2023, we established Anhad Naad Foods (ANAAD) on farm land in Sonipat, Haryana. From day one, we made a straightforward decision: zero synthetic pesticides, zero chemical fertilizers, and zero hybrid seeds engineered just for shelf life.

Instead, we practice Indigenous Cow-Based Natural (ICBN) farming. We keep Desi Gir cows right on the farm. Their dung and urine are used to prepare traditional Jeevamrit and Ghanjeevamrit, which feed the natural soil bacteria and earthworms. We also revived native seeds like Desi Khapli (Emmer wheat) and Sona Moti—ancient grains that are naturally lower in gluten load, higher in fiber and minerals, and far easier to digest.

Our Commitment

Three practical rules we stick to:

01
Milled after you order, delivered in 72 hours

We don't keep pre-milled bags sitting in a godown. Your grains stay in whole-seed form until you place an order or your subscription cycle begins. We stone-grind on traditional chakkis running below 60 RPM to keep the flour cool, pack it immediately, and dispatch it to your doorstep within 72 hours.

02
Real batch traceability on every pack

You don't have to take our word for it. Every packet has a Batch QR code. Scan it to see the exact plot in Sonipat where it was harvested, the farmer who grew it, and independent third-party SGS lab test reports confirming zero chemical residues.

03
Food that truly nourishes: “जैसा अन्न, वैसा मन”

Our elders always said what you eat shapes how you feel and think. When your daily grain is grown without chemical poisons and eaten fresh, you notice the difference right away in your digestion, energy, and overall health.

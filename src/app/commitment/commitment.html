Share the risk. Secure the harvest.

In conventional industrial farming, growers often decide what to sow by predicting what the market might buy. When that prediction fails, prices fall, produce is devalued, and food goes to waste. ANAAD's subscribers commit before sowing, giving the farmer the confidence to grow only what already has a home while adhering to the ICBN agricultural practices.

A subscription is not just a recurring order. It funds a part of the next sowing cycle in advance. In return, subscribers receive staples directly from the source (without going through 7-8 levels of middlmen), at a price fixed before harvest, from a crop their commitment helped bring to life.

Why It Matters

The moment you buy food off a supermarket shelf, you have already lost.

By the time conventional food reaches your kitchen, it has already passed through traders, warehouses, processors, and months of storage.Every step rewards shelf life and volume, not on our planet, the farmer or your health.

Commitment-Based Agriculture reverses that journey. We begin with your household instead of ending there. Your food is grown for you before it exists, not sold to you after it has reached the supermarket shelf.

The Process

How your subscription works

Four steps. One season. A relationship that grows stronger over time.

01

Choose Your Plan

Select the Subscription duration that fits your household. Every plan includes full batch ledger access and our unconditional zero-residue guarantee based on industry leading EU standard.

02

Your Allocation Is Reserved

When you subscribe, a portion of the harvest is reserved in your name for the duration of your subscription.

03

Harvest, Test, and Report

Once your grain is harvested, regular batch testing is conducted. You can view the batch reports on our website by scanning the QR code on the package.

04

Milled and Dispatched to You

The grain is milled into flour during scheduled milling cycles exclusively for our subscribers. Once milled, it is dispatched to your door.

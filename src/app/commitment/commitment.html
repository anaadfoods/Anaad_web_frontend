Why It Matters

The moment you buy grain off a supermarket shelf, you have already lost.

Conventional retail forces farmers to grow for volume, shelf-life, and margin — not nutrition. By the time flour reaches a supermarket shelf, it may have spent months in a warehouse, milled months before that, from grain grown under conditions that prioritise yield over quality. Commitment-Based Agriculture inverts this entirely.

The Process

How your subscription works. No ambiguity.

Four steps. One season. A relationship that compounds.

01

Choose Your Tier

Select the monthly quantity that suits your household. Every tier includes full batch ledger access and our unconditional zero-residue guarantee.

02

Your Allocation Is Reserved

Your portion of the upcoming harvest is entered into our farm records before sowing begins. We adjust the sowing area based on total committed subscriptions. Your demand shapes what we plant.

03

Harvest, Test, and Report

Once your grain is harvested, it goes to SGS India before anything else happens. You receive the batch report via email before your flour is dispatched. You see the data before the bag arrives.

04

Milled and Dispatched to You

Cold stone-milled within 14 days of harvest clearance. Nitrogen-flushed. Dispatched to your door within 72 hours of milling. Auto-repeat every 30 days until you say otherwise.

Questions

Common questions.

If your question is not answered here, write to us directly at farm@anaadfoods.com — we respond within one working day.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes. There are no lock-in periods, cancellation fees, or minimum commitment durations. Cancel before your next billing date and no further charge will be made. We only ask that you give us 5 days notice so we can adjust our milling schedule accordingly.
What happens if a harvest fails or is delayed?
In the event of a harvest shortfall due to weather or crop failure, we will notify all subscribers immediately, issue a full pro-rated refund for the affected month, and — where stock exists — offer supply from our reserve grain bank. We will never ship a batch that has not passed lab clearance.
Can I choose which variety I receive each month?
Yes. At the time of subscription setup, you select your preferred variety: Sona Moti, Chawal Kathiya, or Sharbati. Tapasvi and Siddh tier subscribers may request a split allocation across varieties each month.
How fresh is the flour when it arrives?
Your flour is milled within 14 days of your dispatch date and shipped within 72 hours of milling. The nitrogen-flushed packaging maintains freshness for up to 9 months from the milling date, but under normal household use you will consume it long before that.
Do you ship pan-India?
Yes. We currently dispatch to all major metro areas and Tier 1 cities via temperature-controlled courier. We are expanding to Tier 2 cities through 2026. Enter your PIN code at checkout to confirm serviceability before subscribing.
What does "named plot stewardship" mean for Siddh subscribers?
Siddh subscribers are assigned a specific field block on our 28-acre farm. That block is cultivated exclusively for their allocation. You receive GPS coordinates, seasonal soil test reports, and photo documentation from your named plot every quarter. Your flour comes from a specific piece of land you can point to on a map.
